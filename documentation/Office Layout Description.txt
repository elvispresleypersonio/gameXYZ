## 1. Character Profile: BossMan

* **Name:** Sanchit Shekhar ("BossMan")
* **Character ID:** `OFFICE_WORKER_01`
* **Visual Appearance:** Voxel/pixel isometric style, short brown hair/balding top, black-rimmed glasses, blue/teal short-sleeved Hawaiian shirt with red floral print, dark trousers, dark shoes.
* **Sprite Directions (8-Way Movement):**
* North (N), South (S), East (E), West (W)
* North-East (NE), South-East (SE), South-West (SW), North-West (NW)


* **Base Stats:**
* **Movement Speed:** 4.5 units/sec
* **Health (HP):** 100 / 100
* **Stamina:** 80 / 80
* **Special Ability:** `COFFEE_BREAK` (Regenerates HP)


* **Inventory Items:**
1. `item_coffee_cup` (Heals HP / Triggers Coffee Break)
2. `item_laptop` (Required for Desk interaction)
3. `item_id_badge_1` (Access card)
4. `item_id_badge_2` (Elevator/Door access card)

## 3. Primary Key Interactive Locations

### Target 1: BossMan's Desk (BLUE MARKER)

* **Location ID:** `loc_bossman_desk`
* **Coordinates:** $X: 42$, $Y: 46$ (Bottom row of south desk banks, left-hand cluster, south-facing desk seat)
* **Object Type:** Workstation (Desk, Monitor, Chair, Docking Station)
* **Interaction Action:** `PRESS_E_TO_WORK`
* **Trigger Event:** Open laptop, check emails, sync code repositories.

### Target 2: BossMan's Unofficial Office — Whelan's (ORANGE MARKER)

* **Location ID:** `loc_whelans_meeting_room`
* **Coordinates:** $X: 11$, $Y: 46$ (Bottom-left corner meeting room)

* **Interaction Action:** `PRESS_E_TO_HOST_MEETING`
* **Trigger Event:** Triggers "Unofficial Boss Meeting", restores Stamina to 100%.

## 6. Logic & Engine Integration Rules

1. **Spawn Point:** Character spawns near Reception ($X: 16, Y: 26$) facing South ($S$).
2. **Core Quest Line:**
* Step 1: Walk to **BossMan's Desk** ($X: 42, Y: 46$). Press `E` to grab Laptop.
* Step 2: Walk to **Small Kitchen Area** ($X: 23, Y: 34$). Trigger `COFFEE_BREAK` to restore HP.
* Step 3: Walk to **Whelan's Unofficial Office** ($X: 11, Y: 46$). Press `E` to start the team demo meeting.


3. **Movement Handling:**
* Smooth 2D grid/pixel collision using bounding boxes around all core walls, meeting room partitions, and desk rows.
